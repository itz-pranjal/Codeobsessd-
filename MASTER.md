## About the Project
CodeObsessed was established to demystify the complexities of the
modern programming landscape by providing structured, transparent
guidance for aspiring developers. We aim to transform the
overwhelming influx of technical information into a coherent
professional roadmap, ensuring that no beginner has to navigate
the transition from theory to industry-ready expertise alone.
